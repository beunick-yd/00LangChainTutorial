TypeDict is a way to define a dictionary in Python where you specify what keys and values should exist. It helps ensure that your dictionary follows a specific structure.

Why use TypeDict?

It tells Python what keys are required and what types of values they should have.
It doesnt validate data at runtime(it just helps with type hints for better coding)
